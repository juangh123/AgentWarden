# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We pledge to make participation in the AgentWarden community a harassment-free
experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity,
sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience,
education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race,
religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected Behavior

- Be respectful, specific, and constructive.
- Focus technical criticism on behavior, code, and evidence.
- Accept responsibility, apologize when appropriate, and learn from mistakes.
- Respect privacy and never post secrets or personal data.
- Avoid promotional, hostile, or deliberately disruptive conduct.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, threats, insults, or discriminatory language
- Sexualized attention or imagery
- Publishing private information without explicit permission
- Trolling, repeated bad-faith arguments, or deliberate disruption
- Retaliation against anyone who reports a concern

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove comments, close issues or pull requests, issue a warning,
or temporarily or permanently ban participation. Enforcement decisions should
be proportionate, documented privately, and avoid unnecessary disclosure of
the reporter.

Report conduct concerns privately to the maintainer, `@juangh123`, through a
GitHub contact method that does not expose sensitive details publicly.
Maintainers must recuse themselves when they are directly involved.
